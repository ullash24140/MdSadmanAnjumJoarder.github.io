## About

I am **Md. Sadman Anjum Joarder**, a mechanical engineer and early-career researcher from Bangladesh. I currently serve as an **Instructor at the Directorate of Technical Education** (9th Grade, 43rd BCS Non-Cadre) and have been recommended for **Assistant Engineer (Mechanical), Roads and Highways Department** through the 44th BCS Cadre.

My research sits at the intersection of **carbon dioxide capture, utilization and storage (CCUS)**, **renewable energy systems**, and **techno-economic analysis** of clean-energy technologies. Through my work I aim to make low-carbon transitions practical for the Global South — using process simulation, technoeconomic modelling and policy-aware engineering to design carbon capture pathways, recycle PV waste, convert agricultural residue to energy, and accelerate grid-connected solar adoption in institutions across Bangladesh.
